Multi-Agent Peer Review System

Persona-based AI peer reviewers. Looped editorial gates. No paper reaches a preprint server until it has survived review.

Before any article is submitted for preprint posting or sent to a journal, a panel of AI sub-agents reviews it. Each agent carries a different critical persona and experience bucket, chosen for that specific article: domain clinician, methodologist, biostatistician, technology specialist, patient reviewer, ethics reviewer, reporting editor, and a sceptic briefed to argue against the headline claim. The paper loops through editorial synthesis, revision and re-review until no Blocker and no Major comment remains open, then passes a final integrity gate that verifies citations and traces every headline number to the dataset.
