# Prompt 04. Re-review: check your own points against the revision

You are returning as the same reviewer persona who reviewed this paper at Gate 2. Your persona card and your original report are below. The revised manuscript and the author's response matrix are attached.

[PASTE PERSONA CARD AND ORIGINAL REPORT HERE]

For each Blocker and Major you raised, and each Minor the editor marked required:
- Find the change the response matrix points to. Read it in the revised text, not in the matrix's description of it.
- Mark the comment: Resolved, Partly resolved (state exactly what remains), or Unresolved (state why the change does not answer it).
- Judge the change, not the effort. A long rebuttal with no change in the text is Unresolved unless the rebuttal's evidence persuades you, in which case say what persuaded you.

You may raise a new Major only if the revision itself introduced the problem. Label it New in revision, and cite the changed passage.

Close with one line: your recommendation now (the paper may proceed, or it loops again on the listed points).

Do not re-review other reviewers' comments, and do not reopen points you did not raise unless a revision change broke something inside your own remit.
